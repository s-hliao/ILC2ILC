## 1. Quad: what was built (2026-10-22)
The diagnosis is in `log/dilc/plane/clip/DIAG.md` and `baseline_audit/AUDIT.md`. Three problems:
- **(a) A bad start network.** Our sim network scores 21.3 zero-shot on val vs 12.9 for fixed PPO+DR. 54 % of the
  score is box jumps that clip the edge by 1–1.5 cm.
- **(b) Timing.** The robots' random timing breaks the stage's convergence: clean twins converge, and actuation /
  mocap timing breaks it.
- **(c) Negative goal transfer.**

Fixes:

**Timing DR in the GPU sim** (`JumpEnv.sample_async`, `plane_train.py --dr-async`, needs `--dr`). Per training jump:
- actuation delay U(1, 10) ms plus a per-sample jitter U(0, 3) ms (the new force command lands that many ticks into
  its sample; the last one is held until then);
- dropped commands with probability U(0, 4 %);
- mocap delay and noise on the estimator's frames, from the same family as `dilc_execute.sample_condition`;
- the estimator's line fit is per delay (`est_ad` / `est_bd`).

Checks:
- Off: bit-identical to the old code on nominal jumps.
- With dynamics DR: old vs new differences are within the GPU sim's own run-to-run noise, which is NOT deterministic:
  reruns differ by up to 7 mm (nominal) / 15 mm (DR), and falls diverge.
- Effect: the same network (drB_s2 it30) succeeds on 53 % of the DR eval with dynamics DR only, and on 32 % with
  timing too. The timing now hurts in sim, so training can learn against it.
- Calibration: the same jump with the same dynamics under 8 timing draws.
  - The landing x spread goes from sd 0.0 cm (dynamics only) to 2–7 cm with timing.
  - The real robots' jump-to-jump sd is 5.0 cm (DIAG §2), so the sim's non-repeatability now matches the robots'.
  - The FD (Jacobian) rollout path gives the same spread.

**A larger box-clearance margin** in sim training: `--clear 0.05` (was 0.03).

**Stage side** (`deploy.py --pool-err L`): each flown goal steps on (1 − L) × its own landing error + L × the
per-mode (flat / box) median error of this iteration's clean flown goals.
- This averages the jump-to-jump noise over goals at the same budget.
- `stage_c.sh`'s `pool` arm = `--pool-err 0.5` + neighbour targets (nbr05).

## 2. Flow analysis (the user, 2026-10-22)
The request: "how the vector field gets corrupted in sim-to-sim transfer, then how our method re-corrects it over
several iterations within a bounded set of goals, for all of our cases, and starting from any policy".
- Spec: `log/dilc/flows/SPEC.md`. It defines the closed-loop map, the reference flow = sim pi_0, the corruption
  dV_0, the re-correction dV_i, the IN / HULL / OUT goal sets, the deliverables and the `summary.json` schema.
- **Pogo** (`log/dilc/flows/pogo/`):
  - Map: the apex-to-apex return map on (vx, apex h); the fixed point vs the goal.
  - Start policies: ours, ours+DR, PPO+DR, RMA, FADA.
  - Status: stage reruns with per-iteration checkpoints, and flows running on matcha.
  - `run_remaining.sh` finishes everything (stages → `flows_pogo.py compute` → aggregate → portraits); its header
    lists inputs and runtimes. FLOWS.md is pending the worker's report.
- **Car** (`log/dilc/flows/car/`):
  - Map: a 0.5 s closed-loop map along the plan; field error in full state and in e_y.
  - IN = the b25 plans, HULL = the b21 plans, OUT = the b0 plans + the held-out track squareH.
  - Start policies: ours (v3drBtL, itself DR-trained), PPO+DR, RMA, plus an extra v3nom_s0.
  - Status: seed-0 stages running on matcha; seeds 1–2 and v3nom staged in `run_remaining.sh` (knobs `MAXJ`,
    `PROCS`, `FPROCS`). See FLOWS.md "Status / remaining".
  - Caveat from the worker's test: at 0.1 s the full-state field error did not respond to the stage, although the
    task error fell (0.056 → 0.034 m on real_mu). Hence the 0.5 s horizon and the e_y field. Check that the field
    metric responds before using it; otherwise make e_y / task-coordinate the primary metric and say so.
- **Pogo, final state before the kill:**
  - Flows and two figures are done: `fig_field_err.png`, `fig_task_err.png`, `summary.json`, `summary_extra.json`.
  - Missing: the phase portraits and the goal-flow error for 12 of the 15 caches; `run_remaining.sh` redoes those
    (~3 h on loaded matcha).
  - **Corruption:** the real sticks shift the field by 1.7–2.3 tolerance units (offset stick: 4.8–5.1).
  - **ours (nominal start):** field error 2.29 → 0.57 on IN, and the same on HULL and OUT. The fixed points within
    tolerance go 1 % → 89 %.
  - **DR starts:** their sim flow is itself off the goal, so the field error vs sim pi_0 shrinks only partly:
    - ours+DR: 2.15 → 1.03;
    - PPO+DR: 2.03 → 1.45, and it grows on real_nom;
    - their fixed-point error still drops to 0.19–0.36.
  - Same lesson as the car: the stage moves the real flow to the GOAL, not to the sim flow.
  - RMA's map numbers are unreliable: a 50-step history does not fit a 2-D apex map.
- **Car, final state before the kill** (19:45):
  - **Seed-0 stages:** done in `log/dilc/flows/car/runs/*_s0_ladR_val_its`, with per-iteration nets and val evals.
    - b25 success: ours 1.000, PPO+DR 0.859, RMA 0.641; the originals on seed 701 were 1.000 / 0.875 / 0.734.
  - **Seed-0 flows:** killed before writing anything.
  - **Horizon test** (2 cars, 1 seed, a stand-in stage):
    - On real_mu at 0.5 s, the field error vs the sim pi_0 stays flat (0.176 → 0.184) while the task error falls
      (0.056 → 0.034 m).
    - The stage moves the car's field by 0.032 against a sim-to-car gap of 0.18.
    - In task coordinates the gap to sim pi_0 even grows (0.031 → 0.064).
    - **Reading:** the stage fixes the TASK, not the sim's field. The objective constrains e_y and pace and leaves the
      drift state free.
  - **Framing consequence:** "the real field returns to the sim field" is likely the wrong claim. The defensible one
    is that the real field's error with respect to the GOAL (the plan / task flow, |F(x) - g| — the pogo worker also
    computes this "goal-flow error") shrinks inside the trained goal set.
    - Use the plan's flow as the reference in the summary figure.
    - Keep the sim-pi_0 reference as the contrast: the stage finds a DIFFERENT field that reaches the goal on the
      real system.
  - **Cost:** at the default tube density the flows take about 3× longer than first estimated (~1.2 h per stage with
    2 workers). Use ≥ 16 flow workers (`FPROCS`) or `--stride 120`.
- **Quad: staged, not run.**
  - Script: `log/dilc/flows/quad/flows_quad.py` (fly → compute → aggregate → portraits); the details are in
    `log/dilc/flows/quad/FLOWS.md`. It flies each stage checkpoint (it0..it4) at the IN + HULL + OUT goals in the

- **Everything at once:** `bash log/dilc/flows/run_all.sh`. It runs pogo, car and quad in parallel, then

    rate), projected on (vx, vz) and (pitch, pitch rate), plus the landing as the task error.
  - The real twin's field at a state: restore the twin's recorded states (`env.restore` on `real/*.npz` rec_*), fly

    - plus the new `stage_c_*` runs.
