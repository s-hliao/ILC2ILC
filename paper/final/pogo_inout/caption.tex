\caption{\textbf{Goal-space generalization at matched and full budgets (pogo).} Normalized error (apex height / 2\,cm,

and a progressive spring (outside the model class); 3 seeds, 95\,\% cluster-bootstrap CI over (seed, stick, goal).
Left: every method at about 11 simulated hours (each baseline from its earliest snapshot at or above ours; the
number above a bar is its simulated hours) and then zero-shot or 36 real rollouts per stick. Right: every method at its
full simulation budget, zero-shot or 36 real rollouts per stick.}
