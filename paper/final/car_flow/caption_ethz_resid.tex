\caption{\textbf{What the stage changes in the policy.} Rubber-tire car on the practised ETHZMobil track (scaled $\times$4.3 to the
F1TENTH, track edges $\pm$0.99\,m from the plan; simulation only), median PPO+DR stage run. At each arc length the zero-shot car's own
state there is shifted across the track; the shading is the stage's change of the steering command at that state, $\pi_k - \pi_0$ (red:
more to the left; the zero-shot row is zero by definition), the arrows where the car is 1.0\,s later, the black line where the sideways
push is zero (the line the flow pulls the car onto). The change grows with each one-lap iteration (mean 0.7, 1.4, 2.4\,deg; at most
16\,deg) and depends mostly on where the car is along the lap, not on its offset: the stage learns a position-specific steering
correction. The line moves toward the plan (10.8, 10.1, 9.7, 9.1\,cm mean offset) and the four perturbed laps from 21.5 to 15.7\,cm rms;
none leaves the track. Three of them pass more than 0.5\,m from the plan at zero-shot (in the corners near 8\,m and 28\,m) and none after
two and three laps; the fourth (starting at 22\,m, 0.30\,m right of the plan) is already moving outward with the zero-shot car's own
lateral velocity there and reaches 0.58--0.63\,m along the same path in every row, before any policy can act. Over nine stage runs the
rubber car's error falls 24.0\,\% (PPO+DR) and 29.0\,\% (RMA).}
