\caption{\textbf{The stage pulls the car's lateral flow back toward the plan on a practised, harder track.} Lateral flow of the rubber-tire
car on the ETHZMobil track (scaled $\times$4.3 to the F1TENTH; 55\,m per lap, simulation only), inside the 0.5\,m tube around the plan,
under the PPO+DR network's zero-shot policy $\pi_0$ and after each one-lap stage iteration $\pi_1$--$\pi_3$ (the median of three stage
runs). At each arc length the zero-shot car's own state there (speed, heading error, sideslip, yaw rate) is shifted across the tube; the
arrows show where the car is 1.0\,s later, the shading the sideways push, and the black line where the push is zero -- the line the flow
pulls the car onto. Orange points: four laterally perturbed laps every 0.2\,s; $\times$: leaves the tube. The flow converges in every
iteration; the stage moves its line toward the plan (mean offset 10.7, 10.1, 9.6, 9.1\,cm) and the perturbed laps from 21.5 to 15.7\,cm
rms, from four of four leaving the tube to one. Over all nine runs per network on this track the stage lowers the rubber car's error by 24.0\,\% (PPO+DR, from 0.65 to 0.03\,s
outside the tube) and 29.0\,\% (RMA, 0.87 to 0.07\,s).}
