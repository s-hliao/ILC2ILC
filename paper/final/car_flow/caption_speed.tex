\caption{\textbf{The hardware stage pulls the car's speed back toward the plan on a held-out track.} Speed flow of the plastic-tire
car along one lap of the held-out peanut (clockwise; never practised). From each point (arc length, speed minus the plan's speed),
with the car on the path and every other state on the plan, the arrow shows where it is 0.4\,s later; orange points are four
speed-perturbed starts every 0.2\,s, the dotted line the speed they settle at. Top: the nominal car (friction $\mu$ 0.6, at which the
network and plans were made) under the PPO+DR network $\pi_0$ (the reference). Below: the plastic car (every friction $\times$0.4/0.6)
under $\pi_0$ and after each stage iteration $\pi_1$--$\pi_3$ (2 laps each on a different track). Shading: deviation of the speed flow from
the reference in tolerance units (10\,\% of the plan's mean speed, 0.23\,m/s). The settled speed rises from $-$0.18\,m/s at $\pi_0$ to
$-$0.16, $-$0.15 and $-$0.10\,m/s (reference $-$0.09) and the mean deviation falls from 0.36 to 0.30, 0.26 and 0.22. Lateral
tracking is unchanged (rms $e_y$ 2.3 $\rightarrow$ 2.2\,cm): on plastic the stage's gain is lap pace (0.920 $\rightarrow$ 0.953).
One stage run (network seed 0, stage seed 9001), one car of twelve; its held-out error falls 41\,\%, the twelve-car mean 27\,\%.}
