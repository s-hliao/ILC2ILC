\caption{\textbf{The stage pulls the car's lateral flow slightly toward the plan on a practised, harder track.} Lateral flow of the
rubber-tire car on the ETHZMobil track (scaled $\times$4.3 to the F1TENTH and run at 1.1$\times$ the plan's speed; 55\,m per lap, track
edges $\pm$0.99\,m from the plan; simulation only) under the PPO+DR network's zero-shot policy $\pi_0$ and after each one-lap stage
iteration $\pi_1$--$\pi_3$ (the median of nine stage runs). At each arc length the zero-shot car's own state there (speed, heading error,
sideslip, yaw rate) is shifted across the track; the arrows show where the car is 1.0\,s later, the shading the sideways push, and the
black line where the push is zero -- the line the flow pulls the car onto. Orange points: four laterally perturbed laps every 0.2\,s. No
perturbed lap leaves the track in any row; the stage moves the flow's line toward the plan (mean offset 9.4, 8.8, 8.7, 8.6\,cm) and the
perturbed laps from 23.3 to 20.5\,cm rms, while every perturbed lap still passes more than 0.5\,m from the plan once (largest offsets
0.59--0.69\,m in every row). Over all nine runs per network on this track the stage lowers this car's error by 11.8\,\% (PPO+DR; time more
than 0.5\,m off the plan 0.37 to 0.17\,s per lap) and 19.9\,\% (RMA, 0.13 to 0.05\,s).}
