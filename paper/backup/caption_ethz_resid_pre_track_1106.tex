\caption{\textbf{What the stage changes in the policy.} Rubber-tire car on the practised ETHZMobil track (scaled $\times$4.3 to the
F1TENTH, simulation only), median PPO+DR stage run. At each arc length the zero-shot car's own state there is shifted across the
0.5\,m tube; the shading is the stage's change of the steering command at that state, $\pi_k - \pi_0$ (red: more to the left; the zero-shot
row is zero by definition), the arrows where the car is 1.0\,s later, the black line where the sideways push is zero (the line the flow
pulls the car onto). The change grows with each one-lap iteration (mean 0.7, 1.5, 2.6\,deg; at most 16\,deg) and depends mostly on where
the car is along the lap, not on its offset: the stage learns a position-specific steering correction. The line moves toward the plan
(10.7, 10.1, 9.6, 9.1\,cm mean offset) and the four perturbed laps from 21.5 to 15.7\,cm rms. Three of them leave the tube at
zero-shot (in the corners near 8\,m and 28\,m) and none after two and three laps; the fourth (at 22\,m, 0.30\,m right of the plan) begins
already moving outward with the zero-shot car's own lateral velocity there and leaves after 0.33\,s along the same path in every row,
before any policy can act. Over
nine stage runs the rubber car's error falls 24.0\,\% (PPO+DR) and 29.0\,\% (RMA).}
