## 2. To run: quadruped landing-model arms (the main open item)

**Question.** The hardware stage barely moves the quad's score (21.3 → 19.7, not significant under any step rule). The
diagnosis is that the landing model is the limit:
- it realizes about 25 % of the predicted improvement;
- its sign is wrong for about 1/3 of goals, even on 5-jump means.

So: does correcting the landing sensitivity, rather than the step size, make the stage work? The arms below are all
on drB_hw24's recipe step settings; only the model part changes.

| arm | flag(s) | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| `recipe` | – | the reference (already run on ube as `recipe_sd900{1,2,3}`; reruns only where missing) |
| `jac_real` | `--jac-at real` | Jacobians with the sim restored to each trial's measured states, not along the sim's own rollout |
| `sens_goal` | `--sens-adapt goal` | Broyden (secant) correction from consecutive jumps, per goal (weight `--sens-w` 0.5) |
| `sens_shared` | `--sens-adapt shared` | the same, one correction per bank plan, shared over goals |
| `sens_vres` | `--sens-adapt vres` | ridge regression of (measured − predicted landing) on the state offset (`--vres-l2` 1) |
| `nbr05` | `--nbr-radius 0.05 --nbr-weight 0.5` | neighbour targets: the interpolation test's best arm, now on 3 seeds × 4 robots |
| `ctl_random` | `--control random` | the causal control: a same-size step in a random direction |

**Smoke test on ube (2026-10-18, 16:13–16:25, NPAR 6):** all 6 new arms started, stepped and evaluated, with no
errors. Outputs are in ube's `log/dilc/plane/smoke_landing`.
- **Final scores** (2 goals × 2 iterations, real_r1 only; 2 prediction pairs each, so this is **not a result**):
  - jac_real 26.0;
  - sens_goal 24.0;
  - sens_shared 24.1;
  - sens_vres 36.5;
  - nbr05 26.3;
  - ctl_random 26.1.
- **The only hint:** `jac_real` was the one arm whose predicted landing change agreed with the measured one (cos 0.88,
  against −0.2 to −0.6 for the others).

**How to read it:**
- **The model fixes work** only if an arm beats `recipe` on the paired difference with a CI below 0, **and** its "cos
  med" column rises above the recipe's.
  - cos med is the median cosine between the predicted and the measured landing change.
  - The recipe's is 0.69 on single jumps. Positivity needs it reliably > 0.
- **`ctl_random` is the floor.** An arm that does not beat it shows no ILC effect on the validation goals.
- **`nbr05` vs `recipe`** decides whether the 1-seed interpolation result was real.
- **If nothing beats the recipe,** the paper describes the quad as limited by its landing model, with ρ and cos as
  the evidence (theory remark `rem:posdomains`).
