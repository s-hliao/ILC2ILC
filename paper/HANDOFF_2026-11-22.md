## 3. How the zero-shot could fail the experiment (framework agreed in the session, written before the result)

The zero-shot's job is to give the stage a **real, repeatable, correctable** error, larger than the noise and comparable
to the sim's prediction. Ways it fails that, and the move for each:

| case | sign | next move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 too good | lap rms below about 10-12 cm: no headroom | PPO zero-shot on rubber (sim 34 cm); else report rubber as "small gap", lean on plastic |
| 2 too bad | wall/spin stop in laps 1-2 | don't stage from it; fallback `peanut125`/`peanut120`, new zero-shot |
| 3 wrong kind | steady offset, not speed/turn error | in sim a steady error was NOT reducible (gentle-launch plastic); calibration (`steer_cal.sh`, frame) before staging |
| 4 not repeatable | large lap-to-lap scatter | 2 runs per condition, battery controlled |
| 5 pace far off | pace below about 0.7 | the stage horizon is plan time -> trials under-used; lengthen the trial window first |
| 6 stage underperforms | (later) | the final eval must be flown exactly like the zero-shot: same start, 5 laps, similar battery |

Suggested thresholds to fix **before** looking at numbers: lap rms 15-45 cm, completes 5 laps, pace 0.7-0.9, laps consistent,
not bias-dominated -> stage. Runs per condition: 1 (runbook) or 2 (my suggestion; the quad result's main caveat is n=1).
**The user has not decided either.**

**This run** passes cases 1, 2 and 4 (large, no crash, very repeatable). It is **case 3** (a steady offset, but physical,
from steering saturation; the slow lap rules out calibration) and **case 5** (pace 0.53).

## 4. Why the stage must not run as configured, and the next moves

1. **Horizon (blocking).** `hw_stage.py` sets the GN horizon to the plan's time: T = ceil((trial_laps + 0.05) x plan
   lap_time / DT) = 2.05 x 2.553 s, about 5.2 s (memory `stage-horizon-plan-time`, the 11-09 foam-oval lesson). A real lap
   takes 5.9 s plus about 1.5 s of launch, so **each 2-lap trial feeds less than one lap, mostly the launch, into the
   update**, and the 8-lap budget is mostly wasted. Fix: a longer trial window for this track (as `STEADY=1` /
   `--trial-laps 2.35` did for the oval) so T covers the real 2 laps. Verify it by replaying `res_00000` through the stage
   code: no driving, and count the samples that survive the 0.5 m/s mask.
2. **Sim check at the real grip (recommended before flying the stage).** Replay the RMA zero-shot policy in sim at lower
   friction (e.g. with `inspect_runs_1119.py` and the hardware launch) to find the friction that reproduces 49 cm / pace
   0.53 / about 45 % saturation / +47 cm bias. Then run the sim stage at that friction (3 seeds, as for the 11-19/20
   screens). If it still predicts a clear gain, stage x1.5 with a quotable prediction. If not, use the fallback.
3. **What the stage can and cannot do here.** The steering is already saturated, so the stage can only remove the offset by
   **braking earlier and harder for the turns** (the pace row in the objective is at scale 1.0). That is a legitimate gain,
   but partly from slowing down, and a reviewer will notice. Report rms e_y *and* pace for the zero-shot and the final eval.
4. **Fallback:** `peanut125` / `peanut120` (accepted by `stage_track_1116.sh`; sim zero-shot 12 cm / 8.9 cm). Lower speed
   may keep the car out of saturation, at the cost of a smaller zero-shot error.
5. **Safety margin:** the peak was 0.85 m against the 1.0 m `wall` stop. A stage step that pushes speed up could trip it.
6. **Plastic** (PPO, rotated R1.0 figure-8 `fig8r100`) is not flown yet. Expect the same grip question; do a slow lap first.
