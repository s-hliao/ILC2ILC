## Method (gps_core.py; checked against the MDGPS paper PDF, Algorithm 3 / Appendix B, and the released code github.com/cbfinn/gps)
Per iteration, per goal (initial condition): S = 5 samples (the paper's 5 per initial state) from the current network + Gaussian
action noise (std 0.05 normalized; the paper: samples from p_i or pi_theta), all counted as real laps / rollouts; time-varying
linear dynamics fitted per step by Bayesian linear regression toward the SIMULATOR's linearization at the mean sample state
(n0 = dx + du pseudo-samples; the paper uses a GMM prior over its own samples: replaced by the sim prior, user's option b, as every
other arm starts from the simulator); the global policy's linearization analytic (allowed by the paper); C-step = max-entropy LQR
on l/eta - log pibar with eta by the code's geometric bracketing until |KL - eps| < 0.1 eps, eps = kl_step 0.2 x step_mult x T;
cost = our objective's error rows and scales (the task rows; the same as every arm); step_mult by the code's 'laplace' rule
(new_mult = pred / (2 max(1e-4, pred - act)) clipped to [0.1, 5]; step_mult in [0.01, 10]); S-step = precision-weighted
least squares of the network mean onto the local controllers' means at every valid sample state, the code's defaults (5000 Adam
steps, lr 1e-3, batch 25, weight decay 0.005), current iteration's samples only. No anchor, rollback or trust region of ours.
Smoke test (one plastic car, 2 iterations): KL hit eps within 10 %, laplace cost 751 -> 695 (predicted 693), step_mult x 5.
